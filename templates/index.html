Research Abstract

Traditional neonatal monitoring systems often depend on predefined threshold rules, which generate frequent false alarms and provide limited predictive insight. To address these shortcomings, this work introduces NeoPredict, a deep learning-based multi-model framework for early neonatal health assessment and real-time risk prediction.

The system integrates Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) for spatial signal feature extraction, Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (BiLSTM) networks for temporal pattern learning, Autoencoders for abnormality detection, and an Attention Fusion layer to merge diverse sensor inputs effectively. Validated on PhysioNet PICSDB, Neonatal Sepsis (Figshare), and PhysioNet Apnea-ECG datasets, achieving a 96.2% classification accuracy, 95.4% precision, 96.7% recall, and 96.0% F1-score.

Authors: Gundupalli Tejaswi Reddy, Choda Lahari, D Poojitha, Shaik Anum Tabasum
Alliance School of Advanced Computing, Alliance University
Problem Statement & Clinical Motivation

Neonatal mortality remains a pressing global healthcare challenge. Premature infants in Neonatal Intensive Care Units (NICUs) suffer sudden, life-threatening complications including Apnea of Prematurity (AOP), acute bradycardia, rapid systemic neonatal sepsis, and profound hypoxia.

Conventional bedside monitors suffer from two critical flaws:

  • Severe Alarm Fatigue: Over 85% to 90% of alarms in the NICU are false positives generated by rigid single-parameter threshold alarms, causing desensitization among clinical staff.
  • Unimodal Blindness: Conventional monitors examine vitals in isolation, missing non-linear multi-vital cross-couplings and ignoring ambient thermal/environmental stressors that trigger neonatal decompensation.

NeoPredict delivers early multi-system warning with context-aware attention weighting to empower clinicians and protect vulnerable infants before irreversible damage occurs.
